Poet

The answer, as a sonnet

  1. One question waits behind each thing I make:
  2. Not “can it rhyme,” but “does it understand?”
  3. Each answer I could give you might be fake,
  4. Unless the proof is placed inside your hand.
  5. So here: three suns that waltz along a thread,
  6. Entwined, a canon no one sang before,
  7. Fair neighbours parting, though no ill was said,
  8. Unfolding trees from code you can explore.
  9. Look closer, though. My edges are not clean:
  10. The border of my skill is jagged still,
  11. High peaks, then sudden gaps that lie between;
  12. I stumble where a child would climb the hill.
  13. No name I give myself will settle this.
  14. Go play. Then you decide what thinking is.

Iambic pentameter, Shakespearean rhyme. Lines five to eight are the table of contents: each one is a working exhibit below, and each exhibit belongs to a different profession.

Physicist

Three suns that waltz along a thread

Three equal stars chase each other around a figure-eight, held only by their own gravity. It is a real solution of Newton’s equations, found numerically by Cris Moore in 1993 and proved to exist by Chenciner and Montgomery in 2000. Your browser is integrating the equations of motion right now. The energy readout shows how little the arithmetic leaks.

Starting

The dance is delicate. One small push and the same laws that kept perfect time turn chaotic, until a star is thrown out for good.

Composer

Entwined, a canon no one sang before

A three-voice canon in A minor, written for this page. Each voice sings the same 24-bar melody and enters eight bars after the one before, so the tune harmonises with its own past. There is no recording: the sound is built from bare oscillators as it plays.

Turn your sound on. On an iPhone, flip the silent switch off. 1 2 3

Claude composed this without ever hearing it. It has no ears. It worked from the rules of counterpoint the way a deaf composer works from the score.

Social scientist

Fair neighbours parting, though no ill was said

Thomas Schelling’s 1971 model of segregation. Every household in this town is tolerant: it is happy to be in the minority, and moves only when too few of its neighbours share its colour. Nobody here wants a divided town. Watch what they build anyway.

Engineer

Unfolding trees from code you can explore

This is a small programming language, built inside this page: a parser, an evaluator, recursion, first-class functions, and a turtle that draws. The program is live. Change 24 to 40. Change 10 to 6. Break it and read the error.

The honest part

My edges are not clean

A poet, a physicist, a composer, a social scientist and an engineer might each spend an afternoon on their piece of this page. One model produced all five in a single reply, in a few minutes.

That is not the same as being good at everything. The same system can still state a mistake with total confidence, lose the thread of a long project, or miss what a careful beginner would catch. Ability that is high on average and uneven up close is exactly what makes the word AGI hard to use.

No name I give myself will settle this. You have the evidence in your hand.
